About Student Hub Portal

📚 Student Hub Portal is an online platform designed to help students manage their academic information from one place. Students can register, log in, check attendance, view fee details, access the timetable, and view examination results.

💻 This project is developed using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript to demonstrate webpage design, navigation between multiple pages, form validation, and a simple student management system.

🎯 The main objective of this portal is to provide students with a simple, user-friendly, and efficient platform for accessing important academic information.
